URCHIN SKIPPER 3D — HOW TO PLAY

Skipper the boat while two autonomous divers collect urchins. Start with Frank's tutorial and Easy difficulty.

Buy a boat, choose Sail and a nearby ground. Use the chart and sounder to find sheltered seabed around 5-20 m deep. Set each diver’s Orders, then press Deploy / board. An available diver aboard deploys even when the other portrait is selected.

Watch bubbles and orange floats. Bring the boat's port side (left facing the bow) slowly alongside a float, clear of the hull and stern. Press Bag once and stay alongside: the catch comes aboard and a fresh bag sends that diver back down. If they cannot continue, their bubble tells you why. Deploy/board instead brings the nearby diver aboard with any bag. Neither pickup action needs portrait selection; selection chooses between available aboard divers and sets individual orders.

Recover both divers before leaving. Cross the harbour edge shown on the chart; follow its Leave by time to reach offload by 19:00. Catch sells automatically; refuel, rest and upgrade.

Sailing with an unavailable diver and sleeping/resting away today now ask for confirmation. Later coasts offer better quality and more unmarked ground; Frank warns you to upgrade your boat when you buy access. Taxis make straight runs at bubbles, so keep surfaced divers close and recover promptly.

DEVELOPMENT TOOLS
Accounts → Borrow up to $5,000 can be repeated without a total cap while the game is in development; debt and interest still apply. Pause → Debug mode → Godmode prevents new boat damage, diver injuries and fuel use. It starts off, saves your choice and shows a banner when enabled. Existing losses, current, weather and diving readiness still apply.

DEFAULT KEYBOARD
W/S throttle; A/D rudder; Space neutral; Enter centre rudder.
Tab select diver; O orders; 1 deploy/board; 2 bag/send down; 3 recall near bubbles.
M chart; +/- zoom; Escape menu.

DEFAULT XBOX CONTROLLER
Left stick up/down throttle; right stick left/right rudder; D-pad left/right neutral.
LB select diver; B orders; Y deploy/board; X bag/send down; A nearby recall.
L3 chart; triggers zoom; Menu pause.

TOUCH
Open Touchscreen Options and enable touch mode; use the labelled sticks and buttons.

Throttle and rudder stay set when released. Neutral does not stop drift. Follow on-screen prompts if an action is unavailable. Controls can be remapped in Settings.

NEW WORKING-DAY OPTIONS
Before sailing, visit Harbour office → Buyer market to choose an optional
quality/volume order. Pause → Deck catch shows each recovered bag’s quality
and lets you dump it overboard over its original hauling time. Pause →
Equipment switches controls fitted gear; enabled boat lights come on after
dark. Local Chart can save a recent diver sample as a personal mark and show
its bearing/distance. Gameplay Speed now defaults to +50%.

Crew report new sampled quality and slow picking, especially on unmarked ground. Set a minimum quality and maximum bag time in Orders. Repeated unchanged reports stay quiet; crew have different chatter habits. Fatigue builds during work and mostly clears overnight.

Home Coast storms are weak enough for the loaded starter rudder boat to reverse into deeper water. Later coasts get much worse: check Forecast before fishing. Falling tide can leave you stuck; wait on the water for it to rise or Pause → Radio for rescue for a paid tow. Taxis sometimes pass straight through worked water; an actual surface strike can injure or kill a diver.

3D GRAPHICS
Settings → 3D graphics cycles High, Balanced and Battery. These affect visual resolution and shadow detail, never the simulation. Use a browser with WebGL 2 and hardware acceleration.
