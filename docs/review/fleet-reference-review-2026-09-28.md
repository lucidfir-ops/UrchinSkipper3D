# Independent fleet and feedback reference review

September 28, 2026. Read-only visual review of the twelve career raster references in `public/assets/fleet/`, all twelve existing `test-results/equipment-boat-*.png` previews, `test-results/cohesion/fleet-twelve.png`, the supplied `feedback/9-28/` images, and `src/three/fleet-profiles.js`. No implementation or asset changes were made by this reviewer. This is a before-change review; it does not approve later runtime output or animation.

## Fleet finding

The current models share an attractive readable style, consistent rails, ladder, spare tanks and useful working-deck details. However, the twelve source boats are much more distinct than the current models. A trim colour, cabin scale and minor accessories are insufficient for several references. The common forward white box cabin is the most obvious source of sameness. These are source-matching issues rather than a request for photorealism.

Priorities: fix Reef Runner's layout first; make tug red and rounded, RIB collar genuinely inflated, and catamaran houses visibly broad/low; then add source-specific cranes, tire fenders, timber seating and catamaran mesh/solar equipment. Cabin position needs a per-profile field: the inspected constructor sets every cabin centre to `-length * 0.125`. Cabin paint, roof equipment and deck accessory fields should also be explicit enough that sisters are not recolours. Use the source as a visual guide without adding fake installed upgrade capability or changing collision/working geometry.

The individual equipment screenshots are large enough to judge static shape and trim, but cannot establish motor rotation or movement. A still fleet montage is useful for identity comparisons; it is not an animation or physical-input test.

## Supplied interface and water evidence

- The desired career image keeps time, speed, throttle/rudder, fuel and depth across the top, with crew and touch controls below. It removes weather/current boxes, picking legend, harbour-exit banner and oversized aboard status. The boat is clear in the middle.
- The desired tutorial puts Frank directly below the header controls, leaving the centre unobstructed. The pickup screenshot shows simultaneous target status, port label, stern label, port ladder message and diver arrow: these are the information overload the user describes. Consolidate messages above Frank and time them rather than retaining all these labels around the hull.
- The purchase bug is detail content overflowing its panel into the Cancel/Buy row. Both `Cash after fitting` and `Available` overlap buttons. A real narrow-screen layout must reserve button space and keep the full cost text inside normal/scrollable content flow.
- The screenshot named `chandlery looks great - please use red circle.jpg` asks for a red equipment-location highlight; the present hauler indicator is yellow.
- The three first-boat images directly demonstrate incorrect identity reuse: blank Harbour Workhorse with its raster under Island Tender, then correct pair, then Coastal Workhorse under Harbour Workhorse. Replace the raster path rather than treating this as only a timing/retry issue.
- The odd wake is a train of large complete pale ellipses, apparent as separate loops behind the stern. These look synthetic; broken expanding wake crests and irregular foam would read more naturally.
- Current arrows are a small regular ring/grid around the vessel. The desired world field must stay fixed to water coordinates and continue across the wider view to make eddies readable.

No conclusions about new kelp/boulder tide behaviour, whitecap animation, or input handling can be drawn from these existing stills. Those need fresh runtime captures and functional evidence.
