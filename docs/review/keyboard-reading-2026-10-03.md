# Keyboard access to menu reading panes — October 3, 2026

The production build at `efbda47` exposed a functional keyboard-access gap behind the forecast's vague scrolling hint. At 844×390, its reading pane contained 507 px of content below the viewport. Native Tab reached the pane in Chromium, but Page Down and End were cancelled by the game's general keyboard handler. Scroll position stayed at zero. The same general handler cancelled navigation on other text panes, and the almanac additionally forced hidden overflow.

The reproduction used a fresh browser context and staged only the initial forecast menu. All Tab, Page Down and End presses were native keyboard events. Its event trace and screenshot are preserved under `test-results/keyboard-reading-2026-10-03/before.json` and `before-keyboard-scroll.png`.

The change gives actual menu reading panes named, focusable regions with a visible focus treatment. Native arrows, page keys, Home/End and Space operate only while the reading region itself has focus. Enter no longer activates a different selected menu button from a focused text region. Nested buttons, text fields, sliders and remapping retain their existing behavior. The same keys retain their established helm commands on the water. Same-screen rerenders preserve reading focus without moving the scroll position; menu history retains its existing recorded scroll state. The keyboard forecast hint remains visible beside the action controls even when the status area displays a save notice.

Independent screenshots exposed two related almanac defects: the portrait view squeezed the graph and chart into narrow columns, and the short landscape view clipped the chart's final notes. Non-touch portrait now stacks both sections in one reader; wider views give each column its own reader. The right stick scrolls the actual reading sections together. A focus-aware Orders hint distinguishes native text scrolling from command shortcuts. Focused Frank buttons also retain native Enter/Space activation, while Tab at sea retains its established next-diver binding; keyboard-only equipment continuation is available through the pause menu.

`scripts/keyboard-reading-review.js` uses a staged saved career at sea, then native Tab/Enter to start, open Weather, read its text, return through history, open the almanac and edit its real slider. It checks the chart's final notes are reachable, the portrait chart retains useful width and a synthetic controller scrolls each actual reader. It also reads Orders without changing the draft, returns to water and verifies Page Up/Page Down still command full ahead/reverse. No test operation assigns focus or scroll positions.

The final production run passes in Chromium and Firefox at desktop, compact and portrait sizes. Receipts and captures are under `test-results/reading-training-handoff-2026-10-03/keyboard`. The fixture follows DOM order toward the slider and waits for native Home scrolling to finish; earlier Firefox failures from navigating away from the slider and checking an unfinished scroll are retained in the review history. Two separate reviewers accepted the corrected presentation, retaining small compact labels and the simultaneous menu-selection/reading-focus outlines as limitations.
