## Changes

Debug time uses the same 60 Hz simulation steps as before, divided into approximately 8 ms batches with a browser frame between batches. The modal paints before simulation starts. It displays the running clock, destination, actual step progress and a clock hand; “Stop here”, keyboard Escape and controller Back keep the simulated progress so far. Repeated activation is blocked, normal helm/menu input cannot queue behind the operation, emergencies still stop it, held-clock fixtures are restored, and the final state is saved. Background autosave waits for the job to finish. Clock progress counts completed steps, including a midnight crossing, rather than subtracting two wrapped clock labels.

The two additive oval planes have been removed. Two neutral-white physical spotlights sit at forward and port roof fixtures and follow the boat’s complete pose. Their world position, direction, cone and operating intensity also drive the ocean shader. Water lighting uses distance attenuation, animated wave normals, a dielectric specular response, low green backscatter and a thin boundary-layer scattering approximation. Real geometry receives the lamps, with small shadow maps. Disabled equipment removes both the geometry illumination and water contribution. Fixture lenses switch with the lights; red and green navigation lights remain.

Equipped diver torches produce short, attenuated underwater beams and illumination of nearby geometry. They fade through the existing water-column visibility and disappear at five metres or outside the actual sight range. Already-permitted bubbles have a restrained brighter green-white tint after dark when torches are enabled; no new hidden diver, catch or ground information is revealed. The original two-diver limit is unchanged.

Vegetation keeps its fixed habitat and roots. Per-plant visual state retains direction, angular velocity, extension and trailing-tip direction. Slack water relaxes the canopy; reversal first reduces extension, swings the float and then lets the trailing blades catch up. Vertex interpolation smooths sampled local-current poses. Curved ribbon paths preserve length through gathering, instead of compressing their waviness into zigzags. Tide changes the rooted plant’s position within the absorbing water column and whether its finite stipe can reach the surface. The representative cove retains 637/262/0 surface-reaching plants at tides −1/+2/+5 m. This is a presentation approximation, not a fluid or structural kelp simulation.

The former maximum (2× the original pace) is now the default 0% adjustment. The range is −100% to +100%, with −100% paused, −50% the original pace and +100% 4× the original pace. A new versioned browser preference migrates old saved values to preserve their actual speed. The complete world still scales together; training's multiplier remains separate. The landscape explanation has been shortened to fit.

The fog veil now uses continuous extinction from the boat outward, with illumination-adjusted tint and full concealment at the existing visibility boundary, softening the hard central donut without extending gameplay sight distance.
