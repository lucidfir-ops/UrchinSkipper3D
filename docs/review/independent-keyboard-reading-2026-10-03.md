Reviewer: `visual_review`, separate from the keyboard-reading implementation author. This initial review inspected 25 actual baseline/Chromium/Firefox screenshots in `test-results/keyboard-reading-2026-10-03`, covering desktop 1280×800, compact 844×390 and portrait 390×844 forecasts, almanacs and Orders. The production receipts from port 5186 report passes in both engines. I reviewed their recorded focus/scroll results, but did not independently rerun the keyboard fixture or test a physical device.

The change makes the reading region identifiable with a clear gold outline. Forecast text has good contrast, and the paired focused/End images show the lower days, coast conditions and final advice reachable while the almanac action remains visible. The keyboard scrolling hint stays on screen alongside “Career saved.” Desktop and portrait Orders retain readable instructions and actions; End exposes Apply, No preference and Cancel in the short landscape view. Chromium and Firefox have closely matching layout, with expected native slider styling differences.

The cropped first row in forecast End captures and the cropped compass at the top of portrait Orders End captures are ordinary scroll positions, not lost content. Compact almanac focused captures also begin after native focus has scrolled its left pane: the receipts record approximately 115–116 px, so the absent graph above is not evidence that the graph has vanished. End reaches its lower action row. Both receipts retain paused world/order state and restored helm commands; those are fixture results rather than conclusions from still images.

Three issues remain in this intermediate build and warrant follow-up before approval:

1. **Portrait almanac readability.** `chromium-portrait-almanac-focused.png` and its Firefox counterpart keep the graph and map in narrow side-by-side columns. Graph axes and map labels are extremely small at the actual 390 px width, while “0.20 m” wraps onto two lines. The data fits technically, but the chart does not read comfortably at this size.
2. **Compact almanac right-hand caption.** Both `*-compact-almanac-end.png` images visibly cut the chart caption at the lower edge; the area name and reference/home-return detail visible on desktop are absent. This is separate from scrolling the left forecast pane. The current receipts prove only that left pane's range, so they do not establish keyboard access to the clipped right-hand content.
3. **Orders instructions versus reading focus.** `*-orders-focused.png` still instructs PageDown/PageUp to change quality and Enter to apply. While the outlined reading region has focus, the verified behavior intentionally scrolls with the page keys and leaves Enter inert. A visible explanation of reading focus and how to reach the action controls is needed to reconcile these instructions. The Back control also retains a strong selected outline while the reading region is focused, so the contextual explanation matters.

Compact text and footer hints remain small even where readable. The new outline sits very close to the leftmost text, but I found no separate overlapping controls or unreachable forecast/Orders action in the inspected pairs. These findings are scoped to the intermediate captures, not final visual approval. Root confirmed the three follow-ups and assigned them to the implementation author; revised captures require another review.

This review does not establish touch hardware, physical controller, screen-reader or general new-player acceptance.
